# Testing Steamy34 – thanks for helping!

Steamy34 is a free userscript that upgrades rule34.xxx: dark mode, a popup viewer, an easier tag search, downloads named after the tags, following artists and searches, and a phone layout. It's a fan-made hobby project and still in beta – things may break, and that's exactly what I'd like to find out.

## Install (2 minutes)

1. Install **Tampermonkey** in your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Brave, Opera…).
   - **Chrome / Edge / Brave:** Tampermonkey needs permission to run scripts. On the extensions page, open Tampermonkey's **Details** and turn on **Allow User Scripts** – on older versions, turn on **Developer mode** (top right) instead.
2. Open **https://sleazyfork.org/en/scripts/606445-steamy34** and click **Install this script**, then **Install** in the Tampermonkey window.
   (Got a file from me instead? Drag it into a browser window – Tampermonkey shows an **Install** button.)
3. Open **rule34.xxx**. A short welcome tour starts – that's Steamy34.

**Android:** install Firefox, add Tampermonkey (Firefox ⋮ → Extensions), then open the link above in Firefox.

**Updating:** happens by itself – Tampermonkey checks for new versions regularly (or: Tampermonkey icon → Utilities → "Check for userscript updates"). Your settings and history are always kept.

## Worth trying

- Open posts (popup), browse with ← / →, download with **B**
- The search box: tags as bubbles, filters, pinning a filter
- The bell next to an artist in a post, or under the search box → "Following" in the sidebar
- White / Dark / OLED, thumbnail styles (Settings → Look)
- **X** = screenshot mode (blurs every picture – handy for sending me screenshots)
- On a phone: swipe left / right in a post, swipe down to close

## Reporting problems

Post in **#feedback** on the Discord (https://discord.com/invite/Udg9sNTBHb) – ideas go in **#new-ideas** – or write in the **Feedback** tab of the script page (https://sleazyfork.org/en/scripts/606445-steamy34/feedback). Tell me:
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
- your browser (and phone or computer)
- the version (bottom of the sidebar, e.g. "Beta 3.2")

Screenshots are very welcome – press **X** first so all pictures are blurred.

Steamy34 collects nothing: everything stays in your browser. *Settings → Privacy & data* shows what's stored and can delete it.
